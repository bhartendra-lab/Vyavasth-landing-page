Most photo sharing apps in India sell a yearly plan. That makes sense if you shoot forty weddings a season. It makes much less sense if you shoot six, or if a school asks you to cover one annual day, or a panchayat wants photos of one felicitation ceremony. For those, the question is simple: **what does it cost to deliver one event?**

We worked it out for one realistic event: a wedding of **20,000 photos**, kept online for **one year**, with face search for guests. Every competitor price below is from that company's own pricing page, opened from India on 21 November 2026. Prices change, so check the linked page before you buy.

**Disclosure:** we make Vyavasth, so read our numbers with that in mind. We have only used prices each company publishes.

On Vyavasth, one event of up to 20,000 photos costs **₹399 + GST** and stays live for 3 months. Add 12 months for **₹599 + GST**, and the same event stays online for 15 months for **₹998 + GST (₹1,178)**, with face search, album selection, watermark and the Google review prompt included. No yearly plan, no minimum top-up.

## When a yearly plan makes more sense

Pay per event is built for studios that deliver a handful of events a season, and for one-off jobs like a school function. If you deliver every weekend, a storage plan works out cheaper per event. On Vyavasth, 75 GB for a year costs about the same as 16 events bought one at a time, and storage plans include GST and original-quality delivery. See [Vyavasth pricing](/pricing).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to share photos of one event in India?

On Vyavasth, ₹399 + GST for one event of up to 20,000 photos, live for 3 months, with face search for guests. Keeping it online for a further year costs ₹599 + GST.

### Which photo sharing apps offer pay per event pricing in India?

Vyavasth publishes a pay per event price of ₹399 + GST. Photomall sells per event plans from a prepaid wallet with a ₹5,400 minimum. Kamero offers pay per event but does not publish the price. Kwikpic, FaceSnap, FotoOwl, PicPapaya and Samaro sell yearly or monthly plans.

### Is GST included in Vyavasth's pay per event price?

No. Pay per event is ₹399 plus GST. Vyavasth's storage plan prices include GST.

### What counts towards the 20,000 photos?

Every photo you upload to the event, including photos you delete later. A 4K photo counts as 4 uploads. You can add 5,000 more uploads to an event for ₹99 + GST.

### Can the client pay to keep the gallery online longer?

Yes. Before the event closes, the client gets a WhatsApp message with their own link to extend it by 3, 6 or 12 months.
