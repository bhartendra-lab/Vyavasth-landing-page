Once the couple finishes choosing, the photos go to the album designer. In most Indian studios that is one person with a PC, a design tool and a deadline the family keeps asking about. The tool decides how many albums that person can finish in a week.

This post compares four album design tools Indian studios use, from Indian desktop software to international subscriptions.

**Disclosure:** we make Vyavasth, which runs album selection before the design starts. We do not make album design software, and none of these companies paid to be included. Prices are as listed on the pages linked below on 4 November 2026; check the current price before you buy.

## The short answer

- **Karizma** for an Indian desktop tool with Indian wedding templates, at a low yearly price.
- **DgFlick Album Xpress** if your studio already knows it; it has been a studio staple for years.
- **SmartAlbums** for fast drag-and-drop design with online proofing, if you are fine with an international subscription.
- **Fundy Designer** if you also sell wall art and want a one-time purchase option.

## 1. Karizma

**Best for:** Indian studios that want templates built around Indian weddings and a low yearly cost.

- **What it does:** album page design with templates, automatic layouts, auto-collage and auto-alignment, basic photo editing, and RAW and PSD support.
- **Price as listed:** from ₹3,500 a year excluding GST (₹4,130 including GST) on Techjockey.
- **Watch out for:** the dongle. Lose it and you lose the licence until it is replaced.

## 2. DgFlick Album Xpress

**Best for:** studios that have used it for years and have their templates built in it.

- **What it does:** album creation with 800 built-in templates, more downloadable ones, auto resize, auto collage, basic colour correction, and export to JPEG and PDF.
- **Price as listed:** the retail listing we found marks it "no longer available" and shows no price. Ask a DgFlick dealer for the current version and price.
- **Watch out for:** older versions were sold on CD/DVD; check that the version you buy supports your current Windows or macOS.

## 3. SmartAlbums

**Best for:** studios that want very fast drag-and-drop layouts and online proofing for the couple.

- **What it does:** album design with smart layouts, plus Cloud Proofing so the couple can review spreads online. One licence works on two computers, on Windows and Mac.
- **Price as listed:** paid plans were not shown on the plan page we checked; there is a 14-day free trial with watermarked exports and no card needed.
- **Watch out for:** it is billed as an international subscription, so check the price in your currency at checkout.

## 4. Fundy Designer

**Best for:** studios that also sell wall art and cards, and prefer to own the software.

- **What it does:** albums, wall art, cards and magazines, in-person sales tools and client slideshows, with online proofing as a "Pro Enhancement".
- **Price as listed:** Album Suite $250 a year; Pro Suite $290 a year or $39 a month (with a one-year commitment); or a one-time Album/Pro Suite purchase at $449, with Pro Enhancements free for 3 months and then $189 a year.
- **Watch out for:** the monthly plan needs a year's commitment, and the trial cannot export print files.

## How to choose

1. **Indian templates or international styles?** Karizma and DgFlick are built around Indian albums; SmartAlbums and Fundy lean towards international layouts.
2. **Own it or rent it?** Dongle and one-time licences suit studios that hate subscriptions; subscriptions keep you on the latest version.
3. **Does the couple review spreads online?** SmartAlbums and Fundy include online proofing; with the others, you will send PDFs on WhatsApp.
4. **Which computers?** Check every tool runs on the designer's PC before you pay.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which album design software is best for Indian weddings?

For Indian templates at a low yearly price, Karizma. For fast layouts and online proofing, SmartAlbums. For studios that also sell wall art and want a one-time purchase, Fundy Designer.

### Is album design software a one-time purchase?

Some is. Fundy Designer offers a one-time purchase, and older DgFlick versions were sold as full versions with lifetime upgrades. Karizma is listed as yearly, and SmartAlbums is a subscription.

### Can couples review album spreads online?

With SmartAlbums (Cloud Proofing) and Fundy Designer (online proofing in Pro Enhancements), yes. With other tools, studios usually export spreads as PDF or images and share them.
