For most Indian wedding studios, Instagram is not where clients first hear about you. It is where they check you after a relative mentions your name. A family looks at your profile for thirty seconds and decides whether to call. That changes what you should post.

## The short answer

Make the profile answer three questions fast: do you shoot weddings like ours, are you in our city, and how do we reach you. Then post complete wedding stories, tag everyone involved, and reply to every message quickly on WhatsApp.

## 1. Set up the profile for a thirty-second visit

- **Name field:** studio name plus "Wedding Photographer" and your city. The name field is searchable inside Instagram.
- **Bio:** one line on your style, the cities you cover, and a WhatsApp link.
- **Highlights:** one per type of wedding or function (Haldi, Sangeet, Pheras, Reception, Pre-wedding), plus one for client reviews and one for "How to book".
- **Pinned posts:** your three best complete weddings, not three single photos.

## 2. Post complete weddings, not only best shots

Families want to see that you can cover their whole wedding, not that you took one great photo. For each wedding:

- A carousel of 10 photos that tells the story from Haldi to Vidaai.
- One or two Reels with the moments that move people: varmala, pheras, vidaai, the baraat dance.
- Stories from the day itself, saved to the right highlight.

## 3. Tag everyone, and use Collab posts

Every wedding involves a venue, a decorator, a makeup artist and the couple. Tag all of them, and invite the couple or vendors as collaborators on the post, so it appears on their profiles too. Their followers are people planning weddings in your city.

Always ask the couple before posting, and respect families who want their wedding kept private.

## 4. Use location and city in every post

Add the venue as the location, and name the city in the caption. Families search for weddings at venues they know, and a post tagged at a well-known local venue reaches them.

## 5. Turn DMs into WhatsApp conversations

Most enquiries start as an Instagram message and close on WhatsApp. Reply within minutes if you can, share a sample gallery and your packages, and move the conversation to WhatsApp, where families are more comfortable.

## 6. Let every wedding feed your profile

Guests who receive their photos in a gallery with your studio's name on it often follow you. ## A weekly routine for a small studio

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| After every wedding | Stories on the day, saved to highlights |
| Within a week | Carousel of the full wedding, with tags and a Collab invite |
| Every week | One or two Reels from recent weddings |
| Every day | Reply to every DM and move serious enquiries to WhatsApp |
| Every month | Update pinned posts and highlights with your best recent work |

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Instagram bring wedding bookings for photographers?

Mostly indirectly. Families usually hear about a studio from someone they know, then check Instagram before calling. A profile that shows complete weddings in their city makes that call more likely.

### What should a wedding photographer post on Instagram?

Complete wedding stories as carousels, Reels of emotional moments, and Stories from the wedding day saved to highlights. Tag the couple, venue and vendors on every post.

### Should I post every wedding I shoot?

Only with the couple's permission. Some families prefer privacy. Post the weddings you are proud of and that match the clients you want more of.

### How fast should I reply to Instagram enquiries?

As fast as you can, ideally within minutes. Families often message several studios at once, and the first clear reply with packages and a sample gallery usually gets the call.
