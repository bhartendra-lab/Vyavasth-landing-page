At an Indian wedding, the photos are not for two people. They are for 300 to 1,000 guests, across four or five functions, who all want their own photos on their phone, preferably before the reception ends. That is what a wedding photo sharing app is for.

This list compares the 10 apps Indian studios ask us about most, on what matters to a studio: how you pay, whether guests can find themselves with a selfie, whether the couple can finish album selection in the app, and what each one is built around.

Prices change often, so open the linked page before you buy. For a deeper side-by-side, each app links to its full comparison.

## What to look for in a wedding photo sharing app

1. **Face search.** Guests take one selfie and see only the photos they are in. Without it, guests scroll through 5,000 photos and give up.
2. **Album selection.** After the wedding, the couple and family have to choose 300 to 500 photos for the album. If the app cannot do that, it moves to WhatsApp screenshots.
3. **How you pay.** Per event, per photo count or per GB. The cheapest plan depends on how many weddings you shoot.
4. **GST.** Some prices include it, some add 18% on top, and some pages do not say. Compare the price you will actually pay.
5. **Live upload.** Can you upload during the wedding, from the camera or a laptop, so guests see photos the same day?
6. **Branding.** Your logo, colours and watermark, so every share advertises your studio.

## What about Google Drive?

Many studios start with a Google Drive link, and for sending files to one person it works. For a whole wedding it breaks: popular files get locked, guests scroll folders of file names, there is no face search and selection turns into screenshots. ## How to choose

1. **Count your season.** A handful of weddings: pay per event. Twenty or more: a yearly plan.
2. **Decide who the app is for.** If it is mainly guests, any face search app on this list works. If it is the couple's album, check album selection first.
3. **Price the same wedding in two apps.** Take one real wedding (photos, GB, functions) and work out what each app would charge with GST.
4. **Run one real event on the free plan or trial** before moving your whole season.
