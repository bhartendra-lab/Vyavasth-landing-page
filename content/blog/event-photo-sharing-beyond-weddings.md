We built Vyavasth for Indian weddings: five functions, hundreds of guests, and a family that wants the album yesterday. Then studios started using it for events that have nothing to do with weddings. A sarpanch's felicitation ceremony. School annual days. And it turns out these events need exactly what a wedding needs.

## The sarpanch's samman samaroh

**Shree Balaji Photo Studio** was booked to cover a samman samaroh organised by a sarpanch: a felicitation ceremony where people from the area are called on stage, given a shawl and a memento, and photographed with the guests of honour.

Every person honoured wants one thing from that day: **their photo on stage**, quickly, to send to their family and village WhatsApp groups. The old way meant the studio collecting phone numbers, finding each person's photos by hand, and sending them one by one over the next week, by which time the moment had passed.

Shree Balaji delivered the event on Vyavasth instead.

- **People honoured on stage:** about 60
- **Guests at the ceremony:** about 400
- **Photos delivered:** about 1,500
- **People who found their photos with a selfie:** about 250
- **First photos online:** within 2 hours of the first felicitation

> "Pehle har kisi ko unki photo bhejne mein ek hafta lag jaata tha. Is baar log wahin baithe baithe apni photo nikal kar groups mein bhej rahe the." ("Earlier, sending everyone their photos took a week. This time people were finding their photos right there and sending them to their groups.") – Shree Balaji Photo Studio

On the day, there was one QR code for the whole ceremony. Each person scanned it, took one selfie, and saw only the photos they were in: their moment on stage, the group photos, and the candid ones from the crowd. Nobody had to scroll through everyone else's photos, and nobody had to wait for the studio to send them.

For a studio, an event like this is also the best advertisement it can buy. Every honoured guest forwards their photo, and every photo carries the studio's name and watermark into the groups where the next booking comes from.

## School annual days and functions

Studios have also used Vyavasth for school events, and the fit is obvious once you see it. At a school annual day, sports day or farewell, every parent wants photos of **their own child**, not a Google Drive folder of 3,000 photos of everyone else's children.

How a school event works on Vyavasth:

1. **Before the event:** create one event and print its QR code on a standee at the entrance, or send the link to class WhatsApp groups through the school.
2. **During the event:** upload as you shoot, from the camera over FTP or from a laptop, so photos appear while the function is still on. See [live photo delivery](/features/live-photo-delivery).
3. **Parents scan their child's face:** with the camera on their child, or a selfie for photos of themselves, each parent sees only the photos that face appears in.
4. **After the event:** the gallery stays live for 3 months, and the school or parents can extend it if they want it longer.

It also solves a privacy worry schools have about sharing photos of children: instead of one public folder of every child, each family finds its own photos. Check the school's own photo policy before the event, and tell parents how the gallery works.
