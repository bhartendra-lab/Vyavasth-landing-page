Most Indian wedding studios run their business on three things: a WhatsApp chat per client, a notebook or Excel sheet of advances, and the owner's memory. It works until the season peaks. Then a Sangeet is double-booked, a balance payment slips by two months, and nobody remembers which candid photographer was promised for the Haldi.

A CRM (customer relationship management) tool puts enquiries, quotations, bookings, crew and payments in one place. This post compares three that are built for Indian studios.

**Disclosure:** we make Vyavasth, which delivers photos and runs album selection. We do not make a CRM, and none of these companies paid to be included. Prices are as listed on each company's site on 4 November 2026. Check the current page before you buy.

## The short answer

- **Studio Mithra** if you want a full CRM with proposals, payment milestones and a crew calendar, billed monthly.
- **WedTrack** if multi-day weddings with many events and crews are your main headache, and you want to try it free on 5 weddings.
- **Camtom** if you want quotations, billing and order booking at the lowest yearly price.

## What a wedding studio CRM should do

Before comparing tools, here is what matters for an Indian wedding studio specifically:

1. **One booking, many functions.** Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, wedding and reception are separate events with their own dates, venues and crews, under one client.
2. **Payment milestones.** Advance at booking, a part on the wedding day, the balance at delivery, in UPI, bank transfer and cash.
3. **Crew calendar.** Who is shooting where, so two weddings on the same date do not get the same candid photographer.
4. **Quotations and GST invoices** that look professional on a phone.
5. **Post-production tracking.** Which weddings are waiting on edits, album selection or printing.

## 1. Studio Mithra

**Best for:** studios that want proposals, payments and crew in one place and are happy to pay monthly.

- **What it does:** a lead pipeline for Instagram, website and referral enquiries; multi-event proposals in rupees (Haldi, wedding, reception) shared as mobile links; payment milestones across UPI, bank and cash; a crew calendar with attendance and expenses; and a post-production board.
- **Price as listed:** Studio ₹999 a month (3 seats), Pro ₹1,999 a month (10 seats, GST-ready invoices), Business ₹3,999 a month (25 seats, several brands, custom domain). A yearly option is advertised as "2 months free".
- **Watch out for:** GST-ready invoices start on Pro, not Studio.

## 2. WedTrack

**Best for:** studios whose weddings are many events across several days, with large crews.

- **What it does:** keeps every event of a multi-day wedding under one booking, each with its own date, venue, crew and deliverables; checks photographer, cinematographer and gear availability on one calendar; tracks payment milestones per event and per wedding; follows photo, video, reel and album edits from ingest to delivery; and gives each client a status link.
- **Price as listed:** not shown on its wedding photography page. You can start free with 5 weddings, without a card.
- **Watch out for:** without published prices, ask for the full-year cost before you move all your weddings in.

## 3. Camtom

**Best for:** small studios that want quotations, billing and order booking at the lowest yearly cost.

- **What it does (CRM side):** quotations, billing and payment receipts, order booking with an order calendar, client and team management, payment records, expenses, and order PDFs. It has desktop, Android and iOS apps.
- **Price as listed:** ₹1,299 a year for its Elite Photographer plan.
- **Watch out for:** Camtom is order management software first. Judge it on the back office, which is what it is built around.

## Or keep Excel and WhatsApp?

If you shoot fewer than 15 weddings a season and one person handles all bookings, a well-kept Google Sheet and a WhatsApp Business account still work. The moment two people take bookings, or you run two crews on the same date, a CRM pays for itself in the first double-booking it prevents.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best CRM for wedding photographers in India?

It depends on your size. Studio Mithra suits studios that want proposals, payment milestones and a crew calendar on a monthly plan. WedTrack suits studios with large multi-day weddings. Camtom suits small studios that mainly need quotations and billing at a low yearly price.

### Do I need a CRM as a small wedding studio?

Not always. Under about 15 weddings a season with one person taking bookings, a shared sheet and WhatsApp Business can be enough. Once several people book or you run more than one crew, a CRM prevents double bookings and missed payments.

### Does a CRM deliver photos to clients?

Usually not. CRMs track bookings, payments and work status. Delivering photos to guests and running album selection need a gallery tool such as Vyavasth.

### Can I issue GST invoices from a photography CRM?

Some can. Studio Mithra lists GST-ready invoices from its Pro plan, and Camtom lists billing. Check that the invoice format meets your accountant's needs before you switch.
