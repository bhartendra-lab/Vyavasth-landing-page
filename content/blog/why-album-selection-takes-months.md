Most wedding albums in India are not late because of editing. They are late because the couple has not finished choosing photos. The studio delivers 5,000 to 8,000 photos, the couple promises to select "this weekend", and three months later the studio is still sending reminders on WhatsApp.

The delay is rarely laziness. It comes from how selection is set up. Change the setup and most couples finish in days.

## Why couples take so long

- **Too many photos at once.** Scrolling 6,000 photos on a laptop is a job, not a pleasure. Couples open the folder, look at the Haldi, get tired and close it.
- **Too many decision makers.** The bride, the groom, both mothers and one cousin with strong opinions all want a say. Nobody owns the final list.
- **No clear rule.** "Select your favourite photos" has no end. Couples do not know if they should pick 200 or 900.
- **Selection happens in the wrong place.** Folders on a pen drive or Google Drive are hard to use on a phone, and the phone is where the family actually looks at photos.
- **Life restarts.** After the wedding the couple goes back to work, travels, visits relatives. The album slips down the list every week it is not finished.

## 7 ways to get couples to finish

### 1. Agree the selection rules before the wedding

Put it in the booking conversation, not after delivery: how many photos the album holds, how many the couple should pick, and the date selection closes. A couple who knows "pick around 350 photos for a 40-sheet album by the 30th" finishes faster than one told "pick your favourites".

### 2. Name one decision maker

Ask the couple who gives the final yes. Everyone else can suggest, but one person submits. This single step removes most of the back and forth.

### 3. Split selection by function

Ask for Haldi and Mehendi first, then Sangeet, then the wedding and reception. Smaller sets feel finishable, and you can start designing the first spreads while the rest is being chosen.

### 4. Move selection to the phone

Families look at photos on their phones, in spare minutes. A gallery where they can tap a heart on a photo gets used. A folder they have to download does not.

### 5. Let the family like photos, then you filter

Instead of asking the couple to produce one perfect list, let the couple and close family like the photos they want. The studio then filters those likes: only the couple's picks, the bride's side, the groom's side, or the most liked photos. ### 6. Send reminders with progress, not pressure

"You have chosen 210 of about 350 photos, the Reception is left" works better than "Please complete selection". Progress makes the end visible.

### 7. Never retype a list of file numbers

Every list typed by hand creates mistakes, and every mistake means a wrong photo in a printed album. If selection happens in a gallery, the studio already has the exact files. If it does not, use a reliable way to [find the original and RAW files](/blog/find-raw-files-from-client-selection).

## A selection timeline that works

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| At booking | Agree album size, number of photos to pick, and the closing date |
| During the wedding | Share the gallery link so the family starts liking photos early |
| Week 1 after the wedding | Couple picks Haldi, Mehendi and Sangeet |
| Week 2 | Couple picks the wedding and reception |
| Week 3 | Studio filters likes, shortlists, sends the final list for one yes |
| Week 4 | Design and print |

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should album selection take?

With clear rules and a phone-friendly gallery, two to three weeks is realistic for most couples. Without them, three months or more is common.

### How many photos should a couple select for the album?

It depends on the album size and layout. Agree the number at booking based on the number of sheets and photos per spread, so the couple has a target instead of an open-ended task.

### Should relatives be allowed to select photos?

Yes, as suggestions. Let the family like photos, but decide at booking who gives the final yes, and filter the likes so the couple's choices come first.

### What is the fastest way to collect the couple's selection?

Let them like photos in an online gallery on their phones, and filter the likes on your side. It removes typed lists of file numbers, which are the slowest and most error-prone part.
