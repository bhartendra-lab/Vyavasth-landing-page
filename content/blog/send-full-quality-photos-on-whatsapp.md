Photographers spend hours editing a photo, send it on WhatsApp, and the client sees a soft, blotchy version on their phone. That is not the edit. WhatsApp compresses photos to save data, and most clients never know there is another way.

## The short answer

- For a few photos, send them **as a Document**. WhatsApp sends the original file without compressing it.
- The **HD option** looks sharper than the default but is still compressed.
- For more than a handful of photos, send a **gallery link** instead of the files.

## Why WhatsApp reduces quality

When you send a photo normally, WhatsApp shrinks its resolution and file size so it sends quickly on mobile data. A 20 MB edited file can arrive as a small fraction of that. Fine detail in jewellery, lehenga embroidery and skin tones is the first thing to go.

## Option 1: the HD button

When you attach a photo, WhatsApp shows an HD option. It sends a larger, sharper version than the default.

- **Good for:** quick previews where speed matters.
- **Limit:** it is still compressed, so it is not the file you edited.

## Option 2: send as a Document

Instead of attaching from the gallery, attach the photo as a Document. WhatsApp then sends the original file unchanged, up to 2 GB per file.

- **Good for:** a few final photos, such as a favourite portrait the couple wants to print.
- **Limits:** the client sees a file icon instead of a preview, it uses more of their data, and sending hundreds of documents is not practical.

## Option 3: send a link

For a full set, send one link to a gallery or folder. The client views photos at full quality and downloads what they need.

- **Good for:** previews of a whole function, the complete wedding, or sharing with the family.
- **Limit:** a plain folder link is hard to use on a phone with thousands of photos. A gallery made for clients is easier. Compare the options in [pen drive, Google Drive or online gallery](/blog/how-to-deliver-wedding-photos-to-clients-india).

## Which to use when

| Situation | Best way |
|---|---|
| Two or three teaser photos on the wedding night | HD button |
| A few final photos for printing | Send as Document |
| A full function or the whole wedding | Gallery link |
| Every guest wants their own photos | Face search gallery link |

## Frequently asked questions

### Does WhatsApp HD send original quality?

No. HD sends a sharper version than the default, but the photo is still compressed.

### How do I send a photo on WhatsApp without losing quality?

Attach it as a Document instead of as a photo. WhatsApp sends the original file unchanged.

### What is the maximum file size for a WhatsApp document?

WhatsApp allows documents of up to 2 GB per file.

### What is the best way to send hundreds of wedding photos?

A link to an online gallery. It keeps full quality, works on phones, and avoids hundreds of separate files in a chat.
